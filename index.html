Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OutreachAI

How do you find email addresses? +

We use a combination of proprietary data sources, public databases, and verification APIs to find accurate email addresses. Our data is updated daily and we achieve 95%+ accuracy rates.

Will my emails go to spam? +

No. We have enterprise-grade infrastructure with dedicated IP warming, SPF/DKIM/DMARC setup, and spam filtering best practices. Our 99.2% inbox placement rate is industry-leading.

Can I use OutreachAI with my existing CRM? +

Yes! We integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and 100+ other tools. Your data syncs automatically so your team stays on the same page.

How long does it take to see results? +

Most customers see their first replies within 24-48 hours of launching a campaign. Significant lift in metrics typically shows within 2-3 weeks as the AI optimizes targeting and messaging.

Do you offer training and support? +

Absolutely. All plans include email support, and Growth+ customers get priority support. Enterprise customers get a dedicated account manager and custom training sessions.

What's your refund policy? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your payment, no questions asked. We're confident you'll see results.
