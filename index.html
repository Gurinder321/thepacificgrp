Questions we get before booking a call

Do I own the campaign assets if we work together and I leave?

Yes you own everything that is possible for us to cleanly transfer. We give full access and transparency into the lists, scripts, and all the campaign data. Our email infrastructure may be built for you to own from the start upon request.

Can you integrate with our CRM?

We use third party software to integrate our outbound system with most major CRMs such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, and Close. We are able to two way sync the data.

How much does your service cost?

Our pricing depends massively depending on the scope, volume, and deliverables. Our typical engagements for managed outbound services range from $5,000 - $15,000/m however individual packages are customized depending on needs.

Where is your team based out of?

We are a 100% remote team based out of Seattle, Washington. Our team is located entirely in the United States and Canada.

Is my market big enough for cold email to work?

Typically we see the best success in markets that have 50,000+ contacts, but can work with customers with as little as 20,000 people in market. If you are unsure, we can discuss how large your market is on a meeting.

How many meetings can we get working with you?

Our average customer books roughly 1 meeting per 200 emails sent (usually around 1,000 contacts). Given those numbers we are able to give rough estimates into meeting targets, however results vary widely depending on your competition, market presence, and desirability of your service / offering.
